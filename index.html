Anti-Sleep Detection System

The Anti-Sleep Detection System is an intelligent safety system designed to detect driver drowsiness and help prevent road accidents. It monitors the driver's eye movements and alertness to identify signs of sleep or fatigue. When drowsiness is detected, the system activates an alert, such as a buzzer, to warn the driver and improve road safety. Key Features - Detects driver drowsiness and sleepiness. - Provides an instant alert using a buzzer. - Helps reduce accidents caused by driver fatigue. - Supports real-time monitoring for improved safety. Technologies Used: Arduino, Sensors, Embedded Systems, and C/C++ Project Objective: To develop a cost-effective safety system that improves driver alertness and helps prevent accidents caused by drowsiness. Domain: Embedded Systems | Electronics and Communication Engineering | Road Safety

Smart Helmet and Rover-Based Underground Mine Safety System

Developing a smart worker safety system that integrates GPS-enabled helmets with a mobile rover for underground mine monitoring. The helmets are designed to track the locations of approximately 10 workers and share their GPS data with the rover. The rover monitors environmental conditions such as toxic gas levels, temperature, and humidity to help identify potential hazards. The system aims to improve worker tracking, emergency awareness, and safety in underground mining environments. Technologies: Embedded Systems, GPS, Sensors, IoT, ESP32
